Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-04 · v1.1

This Privacy Policy explains how the Nutryum app handles your personal data. Nutryum is developed and maintained by an independent developer (an individual), who acts as the data controller. Contact: fellipeduarte45@gmail.com. The app is "local-first": most of your data is stored encrypted on your own device; only part of it is synced to our servers to make the app work (account, profile and community features).

Português

1. Data we collect

Account and profile: email and password (the password is stored only as a hash, never in plain text), username, display name, bio, profile and cover photo, region/city/country, social links, and a hash (irreversible code) of your phone number — used only to suggest contacts; we never store the number itself.

Health and activity data (sensitive data): weight and history, body measurements, body progress photos stored on device, biological sex, date of birth/age, goals (calories, macros, steps, hydration), food and meal logs, workouts, and cardio activities — including location/GPS and routes when you use tracking.

Community: who you follow, feed posts, groups, leaderboard rankings, kudos, comments, blocks and reports.

AI assistant: the messages you send to the assistant and the meal photos sent for nutritional analysis, plus a daily usage counter.

Technical data: error/diagnostic reports, product analytics events without PII (for example, onboarding completed or paywall viewed), notification preferences and app settings. Biometric authentication, when enabled, is processed by your device's system and never leaves it.

2. How we use your data

Run the app: log food, workouts and cardio, compute goals and statistics, reminders and notifications.

Community features: feed, follows, groups and leaderboards, according to your privacy settings.

AI assistant: answer your questions and analyze meal photos.

Maintenance, improvement and analytics: diagnose errors, understand aggregate activation/conversion funnels, and improve app stability.

3. Legal basis (LGPD)

We process your data based on your consent, the performance of the service you request by using the app, and legitimate interest for security and diagnostics. Sensitive health data is processed with your consent and for the specific purpose of providing the app's features. You can withdraw consent at any time by stopping use and/or deleting your account.

4. Storage and security

Most of your data stays on your device in an encrypted database (SQLCipher).

Account, profile and community data are synced to Supabase (our backend provider) over encrypted connections (HTTPS) with per-user access rules.

AI provider keys live only on the server (proxy) — the app never exposes them.

No method is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data.

5. Sharing with third parties (processors)

We do not sell your data. We share the minimum needed with providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — backend, authentication, database and image storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — processes the AI assistant messages.
  • Google (Gemini) — processes meal photos for nutritional analysis.
  • Sentry — error/diagnostic reports.
  • PostHog — product analytics and funnels, without name, email, phone number or free-text content.
  • Mapbox — maps shown in cardio tracking.
  • OpenFoodFacts — barcode product lookups.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect — when authorized, reads step and sleep data from your device.

These providers may process data outside Brazil (e.g., the United States). By using the app, you agree to this international transfer, carried out with appropriate safeguards.

6. Community and visibility

Community features are private by default. What is visible to others depends on your privacy settings (being discoverable in search, sharing activities in the feed, and joining leaderboards). You control this in Settings → Privacy and discovery and can change it anytime.

7. Your rights

Under the LGPD you can:

  • Access and correct your data (on the profile screen).
  • Export your data (Profile → Backup), for portability.
  • Delete your account (Profile → Delete account). Deletion has a 3-day grace period to change your mind; after that, your data is permanently erased.
  • Withdraw consent and request information about processing, via the contact email.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. When you request account deletion, there is a 3-day grace period during which you can undo it; after the deadline, server data (profile, community, AI usage) and associated files are removed, and the local device database is wiped on the next app launch.

9. Children

Nutryum is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the effective date below and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after an update means you agree to the revised version.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your data or the data officer: fellipeduarte45@gmail.com.
