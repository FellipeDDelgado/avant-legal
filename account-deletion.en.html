Account and data deletion

Last updated: 2026-09-16 · v1.0

This page explains how to delete your Avant account (Android, package com.avantsociety.app) and what happens to your data. It fulfils the Google Play account-deletion requirement and complements the Privacy Policy. Contact: contato@avantsociety.com.br.

Português

1. Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  • Open Avant and sign in.
  • Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm.
  • The account enters a 3-day grace period. During that time, simply signing in again undoes the deletion.
  • After 3 days, the account and its associated data are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

2. Request deletion without access to the app

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email contato@avantsociety.com.br from the address registered on the account with the subject "Account deletion".

We reply within 7 days to confirm your identity and register the request. From confirmation, the same 3-day grace period applies; after it, deletion is permanent.

3. What is deleted

  • Account and sign-in credentials (email and password).
  • Profile: name, username, bio, profile and cover photos, showcase.
  • The synced copy of your health and activity data (weight, measurements, meals, workouts, cardio, steps, sleep, heart rate), if syncing was on.
  • AI assistant usage counters and per-call operational logs.
  • Support tickets and suggestions sent from the app.
  • The local database on the device is wiped the next time the app opens.

4. What may be kept, and for how long

  • Crash reports (Sentry): up to 90 days, without direct identification.
  • Analytics events (PostHog): up to 12 months, anonymous.
  • Subscription records: while a tax or contractual obligation exists. Payment itself is processed by Google Play; we never store card details.
  • Copies of support-ticket notifications in our mailbox: ask for removal via the contact email if you wish.

5. Delete some data without deleting the account

  • Turning syncing off (Settings → Sync) immediately deletes the copy of your health and activity data stored on our servers.
  • Profile and cover photos can be removed when editing the profile.
  • Before deleting, you can export your data in Profile → Backup.

6. More information

The Privacy Policy (privacy-policy.en.html, alongside this page) details the data processed, purposes and retention periods. Questions: contato@avantsociety.com.br.
